a little note for you ♡

dear zack, ♡

honestly, i don't really know how to start this letter because there are so many things i want to say, and i'm not even sure if i'll be able to put all of them into words. but i guess i'll start with the simplest thing i can say: i'm really glad i got to know you.

it's funny how some people come into our lives unexpectedly, and before we even realize it, they've become someone we look forward to talking to. i never really know how to explain it, but talking to you has become one of those little things that can make an ordinary day feel a little better. sometimes, it's the most random conversations that end up making me smile the most, and i think that's one of the things i appreciate about getting to know you.

i like how we can joke around, tease each other, and be unserious about the most random things. there's something comforting about being able to laugh with someone without having to think too much about what to say next. i appreciate the little moments we share, even the ones that might seem ordinary to you. sometimes, the smallest things leave the nicest impressions, and i think that's something i've come to realize because of you.

i also want you to know that i appreciate you as a person, not just because of how you make me feel. i hope you know that you have your own qualities worth appreciating, even on days when you might not recognize them yourself. you deserve people who genuinely listen to you, understand you, respect you, and appreciate you for who you are. and i hope you always find yourself surrounded by people who make you feel that way.

i won't pretend that i always know what i'm doing or that i have everything figured out when it comes to feelings. sometimes, i can be playful when i'm actually being sincere, and sometimes, i find it easier to joke around than to say what i genuinely mean. so if there are moments when you can't quite figure me out, i hope you know that it doesn't mean i'm not being genuine. i'm still learning how to express myself, too.

but one thing i do know is that i'm happy i got the chance to know you. i appreciate the conversations, the laughter, the little efforts, and all the moments that have given me reasons to smile. i don't think every meaningful connection needs to be explained or defined right away. sometimes, it's enough to appreciate what you have, let things happen naturally, and get to know someone without rushing to figure out what everything means.

i'm writing this because i wanted to tell you something i might not always say properly in our conversations. i appreciate you, zack. i appreciate the person you are and the little part you've come to play in my days. and even if this letter doesn't perfectly explain everything i'm feeling, i hope you can at least understand that the words in it are sincere.

i don't expect you to have a particular response to this letter, and i don't want you to feel like you have to say anything just because i've written all of this. i simply wanted to be honest with you for once, without hiding every sincere thought behind a joke or changing the subject because i'm shy about expressing myself.

whatever happens from here, i'm glad our paths crossed. i'm glad we got to talk, laugh, and create little moments that i can look back on fondly. i think getting to know someone is a lovely thing in itself, and i'm grateful that i got to experience that with you.

so, please take care of yourself, okay? don't forget to give yourself time to rest, to enjoy the things you love, and to appreciate yourself even when things aren't going the way you planned. i hope you continue growing into the person you want to become, and i hope you find plenty of reasons to be happy along the way.

and lastly, thank you for being someone i've come to appreciate. i may not always know how to say things perfectly, and i might still tease you more than i give you sincere compliments, HAHAHAHA, but i hope this letter makes one thing clear: i genuinely mean the nice things i say about you.

i'm glad i met you, zack. that's all, and that's already something meaningful to me. ♡

sincerely,
gigi ♡
